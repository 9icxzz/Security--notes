# CIA Triad Notes

The CIA triad is the foundation of cybersecurity. Many people confuse it with the Central Intelligence Agency, but they are two different things.

CIA stands for **Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability**. These are the three pillars of information security. An organization must protect all of them without putting strict limits on its users.

## Confidentiality

Confidentiality means that only authorized people can access information. For example, permission to see a file or a project, or access to a server.
